# Current product and claim boundaries

Maintained 2026-11-02. README.md is the public current contract;
docs/VERIFICATION.md records evidence and unresolved limitations.

The maintained product is FastAPI plus static HTML/JavaScript. Check performs two
reproducible PubMed MeSH searches: overall disease–method use and early detection.
Map defaults to the 2026-10-30 80,025-paper snapshot, 50×32 axes, 534 checked empty
cells and 425 low-result candidates. 533 count pairs are historical cached values;
only one missing pair was newly queried during that build. Never imply all counts
are fresh. The Library is a separate 60-paper collection with 56 official abstracts
and 135 directory institutions. TF-IDF/LSA scores are relevance, not evidence quality.

The selected pairing, counts, source status, queries and limitations travel into
the brief together. Low counts do not prove novelty, feasibility or effectiveness.
The old 2× claim stays withdrawn; retrospective co-occurrence is not prospective
validation. Method's animation is scripted. Library literal source matching does
not establish scientific truth. Verified collaborator recommendations and general
claim verification are future work. No Streamlit or historical pipeline UI is shipped.
