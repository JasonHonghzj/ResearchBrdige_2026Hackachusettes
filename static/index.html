Walkthrough: the verification gate

A general-purpose model wrote the three claims below from the same two abstracts. The scout checks each one against the source text and deletes what isn’t supported. This runs in code, not in the model.

Draft claims — unverified model output

· Exhaled VOC profiles separated upper-GI cancer from benign disease with an AUC of 0.85.
· Polyamines were elevated in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma versus chronic pancreatitis.
· Breath testing has been validated for pancreatic cancer screening in a multi-centre trial.
2 of 3 claims supported. 1 deleted before it reached the brief.

Source abstract — method half

Patients undergoing upper gastrointestinal endoscopy provided exhaled breath samples analysed by selected-ion flow-tube mass spectrometry. Exhaled volatile organic compound profiles discriminated gastric and oesophageal cancer from benign disease with an area under the curve of 0.85. No pancreatic cases were included in this cohort, and no multi-centre validation has been undertaken.

The third claim fails on two counts: the cohort contains no pancreatic cases, and the abstract states explicitly that no multi-centre validation exists. Nothing in either source supports it.

What is measured, and what we withdrew

The grid was built from papers up to 2018 only. We then asked which flagged gaps appear in 2019–2026 papers — a window the ranking never saw.
